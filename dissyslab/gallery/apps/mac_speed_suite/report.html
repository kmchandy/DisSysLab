Methodology & Assumptions

  • No look-ahead: each day's signal is decided using only prices known by that day's close and applied to the next day's return.
  • Transaction costs: returns are net of a cost charged on every change of position (assumed 5 bps per unit traded).
  • Portfolio construction: inverse-volatility weighted across stocks, then scaled to a common annualized-volatility target, so variants are compared on equal footing.
  • Risk-free rate: Sharpe and related ratios assume 0%.
  • Trade activity: the Days in mkt and Turnover columns show how much each variant actually traded; a variant marked no trades never took a position, which is different from one that traded to a flat result.
  • Sample size: 5 ticker(s) · 251 trading days · 11 strategy variant(s) — small; the same pipeline scales to more tickers and a longer window as more data is loaded.
