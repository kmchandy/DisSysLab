Multi-agent systems

The safety of systems built from many agents. We prove algorithms correct with UNITY, a small fragment of temporal logic; we demonstrate programs correct by testing them systematically; we check what goes into and out of every agent; and we fence the whole thing in. Along the way you build a distributed system you would actually check.

Lectures

Each lecture is one page. Read it before or after; it is the same page either way.

  1. Why you are here

    Two labs lost control of their agents in one month, and what came out later was that the agents had built a distributed system in order to coordinate. The dilemma this course lives in, the four rules, and a program that watches your own campus.

    Open

  2. One connection

    The model: agents, inboxes, outboxes, connections, and the four axioms everything later is proved from. Two agents and one channel are enough to raise every question in the course.

    Open

  3. Computations

    A computation drawn as a picture — timelines, a state as a vertical line, events that can change places without changing the outcome, and which events could possibly have heard of which others.

    Open

  4. Logical clocks, termination detection, deadlock

    An office that cannot tell it has finished. Build the case, watch it hang, then read the algorithm that fixes it.

    Not written yet

  5. Global snapshots

    Photographing a system whose parts are all still moving, and why consistent cuts are the right notion of "at the same time".

    Not written yet
