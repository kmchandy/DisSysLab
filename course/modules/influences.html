What influences what

An event is an action by an agent. This computation has six of them, numbered 0 to 5. The numbers are names; they do not say which happened first, and events 0 and 1 happen at the same time.

Ask of any two events: could news of the first have reached the second? There are only two ways news travels. An agent remembers what it did, so news passes from each of its events to the next. And a message carries news from the event that sent it to the event that received it. Draw those two kinds of arrow and the question becomes a question about the picture: e influences e′ when you can get from e to e′ by following arrows.
