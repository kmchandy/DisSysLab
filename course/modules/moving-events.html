Moving events in time

Six events, named 0 to 5. Events 2 and 3 happen at different agents and neither depends on the other. Slide them: 2 to the right, 3 to the left, until they change places. Each agent's own sequence of actions never changes — only where they sit in time. As long as every message arrow still points forward, what you are looking at is another computation of the same system.
